Sword Soul

A PC-focused Roblox combat project with distinct weapons, soul progression, and a manga-inspired visual direction.

In progressGames & RobloxScope reviewed 2026-10-04

My role

Product direction, AI-assisted development, and iterative testing

Tools & focus

Roblox · Luau · Combat systems · Game design

The idea

Make weapon choice, timing, and progression feel expressive in a compact combat space.

My approach

Iterate on combat choreography, weapon identities, aiming, readable effects, and the surrounding game experience with AI-assisted development.

What exists

The documented build includes 12 selectable weapons, combos, parries, movement, techniques, soul releases, progression, and a Warden boss.

Scope & status

Active development. This portfolio review used the current project documentation; it is not a fresh multiplayer, balance, or release-readiness test.
