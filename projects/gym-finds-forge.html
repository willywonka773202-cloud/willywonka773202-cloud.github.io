Gym Finds / FORGE

A combined gym and fashion shopping experience with a Fit Lab, saved wardrobe, product discovery, and retailer handoff.

PrototypeWeb & commerceScope reviewed 2026-09-10

My role

Product direction, AI-assisted development, and iteration

Tools & focus

Commerce · Fit Lab · Next.js

The idea

Finding gym gear and building a complete outfit takes too many disconnected shopping sessions.

My approach

Bring browsing, outfit building, saved items, and retailer links into one coherent experience while combining inherited Sylistly and Gym Finds systems.

What exists

The combined app includes an eight-slot Fit Lab, shared saved and owned wardrobe, a bag, and local preferences.

Next steps

Validate catalog quality, affiliate relationships, and customer demand before a commerce launch.

Scope & status

Local product prototype. Checkout, retailer inventory, affiliate acceptance, and revenue are not established.
