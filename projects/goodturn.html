Goodturn

An outing-planner prototype exploring place discovery, multi-stop journeys, and a cinematic globe.

PrototypeWeb & commerceScope reviewed 2026-09-10

My role

Product direction, AI-assisted development, and iteration

Tools & focus

Next.js · Cesium · MapLibre

The idea

Planning a group outing means juggling places, pickups, and the order of every stop.

My approach

Connect familiar place-name search to route planning, compare stop order using road times, and make navigation the natural next step.

What exists

The local preview includes nearby search, saved plans, vector maps, route previews, and navigation handoff.

Next steps

Configure production search and routing services, then validate the experience with a small campus pilot.

Scope & status

Current name: Goodturn. Earlier versions were Carpool Optimizer and Outnook. No live traffic, rider matching, or billing is claimed.
