SDSU Life

A local-first school dashboard and Chrome extension that brings Canvas deadlines, coursework, and a daily plan together.

BuiltEveryday toolsScope reviewed 2026-09-10

My role

Product direction, AI-assisted development, and iteration

Tools & focus

Chrome extension · Python · Student workflows

The idea

Coursework is scattered across class schedules, Canvas modules, announcements, and personal tasks.

My approach

Create one daily view with submission-aware assignments and a time cap that turns a busy semester into an achievable plan.

What exists

The extension includes Canvas synchronization, planning, course information, reminders, and backups.

Next steps

Keep the synchronization dependable and validate the workflow across a full semester.

Scope & status

Personal productivity tool; private grades, course content, and student records are not included here.
