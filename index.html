01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTS

Curiosity is the start.
Making is the point.

I’m Will, a Business Administration student at San Diego State University with an interest in entrepreneurship. I turn everyday problems and “what if” ideas into tools, websites, and games.

AI is part of how I build. I use Claude and Codex to move from a product brief to an interface, then test, question, and refine what comes back. The direction, decisions, and follow-through are mine.

This is an evolving record of that process: finished builds, working prototypes, and ideas still taking shape.

05 / A LITTLE CONTEXT

GOOD
QUESTIONS.

01What do you make?+

AI-assisted web apps, workflow tools, shopping concepts, and Roblox games. I start with a problem or idea, explore a useful experience, and keep iterating. The archive includes all stages of that work.

02How do you use AI?+

Claude and Codex help me develop and revise projects. I work on product direction, interfaces, prompts, testing, and iteration. Each case study explains my contribution and the project’s limits.

03Is every project finished?+

No. Each project has a stage: built, in progress, prototype, research, archived, or needs review. “Built” means an implementation is documented; it does not necessarily mean a public launch.

04What are you looking for?+

Part-time work and internships where I can contribute to operations, marketing, customer support, or practical AI workflows while continuing to learn.
