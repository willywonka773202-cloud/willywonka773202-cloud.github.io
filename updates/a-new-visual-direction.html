A new visual direction for the portfolio

A quieter palette, moving wireframes, and a more visual way to explore the work.

The portfolio moved to a monochrome visual direction inspired by Louis Raillé’s site: condensed display typography, fine grids, and a dimensional gallery.

The implementation uses original animated terrain and screenshots from my own projects. The gallery supports keyboard navigation and touch; motion can be paused, and the site respects reduced-motion preferences.

The aim is to give the work room to speak while keeping case studies, project stages, and my résumé easy to reach.
